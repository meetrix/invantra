04 · Complete asset review

Every SVG and PNG appears on light and dark backgrounds, side by side. PNGs display at their actual pixel dimensions. Large exports scroll horizontally so their native size stays intact. White-on-white and dark-on-dark previews intentionally show contrast limitations. Use the white symbol on blue or slate backgrounds.

Marketplace symbol: 60% of square width and 80% of height; 20% side margins and 10% top/bottom margins. Open Sans Semibold is outlined in the SVG lockup and standalone wordmark. Font source and SIL Open Font License are in fonts/. Rebuild PNGs with sh export.sh, or node export.mjs after npm ci.
